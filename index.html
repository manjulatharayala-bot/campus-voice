How it works

1. Student reports with photo, location, priority.

2. Auto-forwarded to concerned Department Admin.

3. Admin updates status. Student gets Notification 🔔.

4. After Resolved, Student gives Feedback.

Emergency = Auto Escalated if not resolved in 24 hrs. High = 72 hrs.
